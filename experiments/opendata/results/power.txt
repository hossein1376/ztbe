## rule of 3 / rule of 30 (independent trials)
claim FAR ≤ 0.01 at 95% with zero errors: ≥ 300 independent impostor attempts (rule of 3); to observe 30 errors at FAR=0.01 (rule of 30: ±30% relative at 90% confidence; attribution to Doddington et al. UNVERIFIED, not in bib): ≥ 3000 attempts
claim FAR ≤ 0.001 at 95% with zero errors: ≥ 3000 independent impostor attempts (rule of 3); to observe 30 errors at FAR=0.001 (rule of 30: ±30% relative at 90% confidence; attribution to Doddington et al. UNVERIFIED, not in bib): ≥ 30000 attempts
claim FAR ≤ 0.0001 at 95% with zero errors: ≥ 30000 independent impostor attempts (rule of 3); to observe 30 errors at FAR=0.0001 (rule of 30: ±30% relative at 90% confidence; attribution to Doddington et al. UNVERIFIED, not in bib): ≥ 300000 attempts
With N users, cross-user impostor pairs = N(N-1); attempts from the same attacker are correlated, so pairs are not independent trials.
