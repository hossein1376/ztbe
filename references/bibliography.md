# ZTBE bibliography (verified)

Pass 1, 2026-10-21. Every entry below was checked against a real record. BibTeX keys match `references.bib`.

**Verification levels**

- **M**: metadata (authors, title, venue, year, vol/pages, DOI) checked against Crossref, the publisher page, or the DOI landing page.
- **A**: also read the abstract or landing page, so the claim in the note matches what the source says.
- **F**: read the full text, or the relevant section, for the specific claim quoted in the notes.
- **UNVERIFIED**: written next to any field or claim that could not be confirmed.

**Links.** OA = an open-access copy (arXiv, ePrint, or the author's site). Only links are stored here. No PDFs are redistributed, because none of the full texts used were confirmed CC-BY.
